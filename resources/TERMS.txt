3. LOCAL EXECUTION & PRIVACY COMMITMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BUIMB Research values developer privacy:
(a) Local Operation: The editor, Monaco code engine, file tree watcher, and
    integrated node-pty terminal run locally on your personal computer.
(b) Zero Secret Telemetry: Bodhi does not secretly transmit your source code,
    terminal inputs, or personal files to BUIMB Research servers.
(c) Local Persistence: Settings, open sessions, and configuration keys are
    stored locally on your device in your user directory (%APPDATA%/Bodhi).
